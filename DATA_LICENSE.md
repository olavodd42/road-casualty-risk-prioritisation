# Data Sources and Licensing

## Department for Transport Road Casualty Data

This project uses road casualty data published by the UK Department for Transport (DfT), covering Great Britain from 2019 to 2023.

Source: [UK Department for Transport — Road safety statistics](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/road-accidents-and-safety-statistics)

Licence: [Open Government Licence v3.0 (OGL)](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/), where applicable to the source datasets.

Attribution: Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

The data has been processed and analysed for this project. Any derived datasets, transformations, metrics, and visualisations should be interpreted in the context of the original source data and the methodology documented in this repository.

The project's MIT licence applies to the original code, not automatically to third-party data or other materials.

Please consult the licence and terms specified on each original dataset's publication page before reusing the data.
